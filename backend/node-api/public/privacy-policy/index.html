Welcome to SyncTune AI ("we", "our", or "us"). SyncTune AI is designed to help creators and video enthusiasts automatically analyze video dynamics and match, synchronize, and export perfectly timed background music.

We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy details our policies regarding the collection, use, protection, and disclosure of personal data when you use our mobile application, web application, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information Provided by You

  • Account Profile: When you register an account or sign in with Google OAuth, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo avatar.
  • Support Inquiries: When you contact our support team via email, we collect your email address and any details you provide in your message.

1.2 Video and Audio Files

  • User-Selected Videos: The videos you select from your device gallery are accessed strictly for AI scene detection, tempo/mood matching, and audio synchronization.
  • Local-First Processing: Frame and audio analysis is performed on-device whenever possible. Video frames are analyzed solely to match tempo and mood and are never used to train public machine learning models.
  • Exported Media: Exported videos with mixed background music are rendered and saved directly back to your device storage.

1.3 Device Permissions

  • Photos / Media Library (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Used to allow you to select videos from your photo library for background music matching.
  • Storage / Media Write (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used to save your rendered, synchronized videos back to your device gallery.
  • Microphone / Audio (RECORD_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): Used to sample, synchronize, and preview audio playback alongside video playback.

1.4 Technical and Usage Data

  • Log and Diagnostic Data: Device model, OS version, app version, error traces, and performance diagnostics used solely to diagnose crashes and maintain application stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To match background music to your video's mood, rhythm, and scene transitions.
  • To enable seamless multi-track synchronization, segment adjustments, and audio variation plans.
  • To sync your project history and preferences across your authenticated sessions.
  • To respond promptly to customer support requests and bug reports.
  • To protect our users and prevent fraudulent or abusive use of the service.

3. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers essential to running SyncTune AI:

  • Google Identity & OAuth: Facilitates secure user sign-in and authentication.
  • Supabase: Provides encrypted cloud database storage for user account records and project preferences.
  • Royalty-Free Audio Providers: Audio tracks are licensed royalty-free from Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com, CC BY 4.0) and Pixabay contributors (CC0). No user data is transferred to music providers.

4. Data Storage and Security

Encryption in Transit: All communications between the SyncTune AI client and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain account data only as long as your account remains active. You maintain full ownership and control over your personal data:

  • You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated project records at any time.
  • You can initiate deletion directly in the app via Settings → Profile → Delete Account.
  • You can also submit a web-based deletion request through our public deletion portal: SyncTune Account Deletion Page.
Google Play Account Deletion Policy Compliance:
Upon receiving an account deletion request, all account credentials, stored project metadata, uploaded assets, and activity history are permanently purged within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

SyncTune AI is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove such information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, new features, or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated revision date.
